Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what personal data test.dev collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

◆Information we collect

Details you send us, such as your name, email address, company and project description, plus basic technical data like browser type and pages visited.

◆How we use it

To respond to enquiries, deliver and support our services, send project updates, keep our site secure and improve it. We do not sell your personal data.

◆Legal basis

We process data to perform a contract with you, to meet legal obligations, for our legitimate business interests, or with your consent.

◆Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us operate, such as hosting, email and analytics, under confidentiality terms, or when the law requires it.

◆Cookies and analytics

We use only the cookies and storage needed to run the site and understand aggregate usage. You can block or clear them in your browser settings.

◆Retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and protect it with encryption in transit and access controls.

◆Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us and we will respond promptly.

◆Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@test.dev
